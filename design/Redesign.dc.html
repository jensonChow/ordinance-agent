回答 · 每句都挂着它依据的条文，指向句子即高亮右栏证据

香港是自由港，一般货物入境不征关税。114 第 114 条的原话是「shall maintain the status of a free port and shall not impose any tariff unless otherwise prescribed by law」。114 注意句末的 unless otherwise prescribed by law —— 关税被禁止，但烟、酒、燃油这类应课税品仍可由普通立法征税，两者不是一回事。114 特区财政独立、收入不上缴中央，所以这些税项决定权留在本地。106 ⚠

CHAPTER III · 第三章

第二十四條

居民的基本权利和义务 · Fundamental Rights and Duties of the Residents

Residents of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (“Hong Kong residents”) shall include permanent residents and non-permanent residents.

The permanent residents of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall be:

  1. (1)Chinese citizens born in Hong Kong before or after the establishment of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region;
  2. (2)Chinese citizens who have ordinarily resided in Hong Kong for a continuous period of not less than seven years before or after the establishment of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region;
  3. *(3)Persons of Chinese nationality born outside Hong Kong of those residents listed in categories (1) and (2);
  4. (4)Persons not of Chinese nationality who have entered Hong Kong with valid travel documents, have ordinarily resided in Hong Kong for a continuous period of not less than seven years and have taken Hong Kong as their place of permanent residence before or after the establishment of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region;
  5. (5)Persons under 21 years of age born in Hong Kong of those residents listed in category (4) before or after the establishment of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; and
  6. (6)Persons other than those residents listed in categories (1) to (5), who, before the establishment of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, had the right of abode in Hong Kong only.

The above-mentioned residents shall have the right of abode in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and shall be qualified to obtain, in accordance with the laws of the Region, permanent identity cards which state their right of abode.

The non-permanent residents of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region shall be persons who are qualified to obtain Hong Kong identity cards in accordance with the laws of the Region but have no right of abode.

* 註

第 (3) 项有释法：1999 年 6 月 26 日全国人大常委会《关于〈基本法〉第二十二条第四款和第二十四条第二款第(三)项的解释》（Instrument 15）。读第 (3) 项时必须一并读它。
